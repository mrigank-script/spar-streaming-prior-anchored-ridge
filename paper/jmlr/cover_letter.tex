Cover Letter - Journal of Machine Learning Research (JMLR) submission

Title: Finite-Cadence Refresh Equilibria for Streaming Prior-Anchored Ridge: A
Closed-Form Forgetting Window and Spectral Correction

Author (sole): Mrigank Shekhar Singh, DLDAV Model School, Shalimar Bagh, Delhi,
India.  Corresponding author: Mrigank Shekhar Singh, mrigank.codes@gmail.com

Keywords: streaming regression, prior-anchored ridge, forgetting factor, drift
adaptation, deterministic equivalent

Dear Editors,

Description and significance.
The manuscript studies recursive, prior-anchored ridge estimation on drifting
streams. It derives a finite-cadence refresh equilibrium of the anchored
recursion: a closed-form tracking lag, an explicit variance-reduction factor,
and, by minimizing the cycle-averaged risk, a closed-form optimal forgetting
window together with a plug-in anchor strength. A random-matrix deterministic
equivalent extends both laws to finite dimension, and rank-deficient populations
reduce exactly to their active subspace. The resulting method, SPAR, calibrates its window and strength online
from streaming residuals and is evaluated on a factorial grid of synthetic
streams, on an hourly electricity stream, and on BCI IV 2a/2b, with the
algebraic core, the refresh chain, and the regret bounds formalized in Lean 4.
The contribution is a closed-form, machine-checked theory of anchored
forgetting, in which the forgetting factor and anchor strength follow from
measured conflict and drift energy rather than from a tuned heuristic.
